Creative Process & AI Disclosure

Please read all of this first part of the disclosure before deciding to turn it down flat.

Every chapter, every paragraph, every word has passed my “perception filter,” trained by half a century of reading fiction to know what works & what's the “uncanny valley.” However, despite decades of workshops, seminars, and trying my hardest, I cannot write believable prose myself; my raw writing always turns out wooden. And it hasn't improved one iota for the attempt in a half-century.

Because of this, my creative process is a partnership:

What I Do: I handle 100% of the world-building, plot outlining, scene development, character arcs, and deep structural and copy-editing.
What AI Does: I use generative AI as a tool to help bridge the gap between my outlines and the raw text, drafting the initial prose based on my exact conceptual directions.
The Final Polish: I hand-edit and proofread every single word to ensure the final story matches my exact vision.

Why I do it this way: I write these so I can enjoy reading the result, just as I would a book by the original author, and because leaving them as un-fleshed-out ideas is unconscionable. The difference is, I finally have tools good enough to do something about it. Posting them here will always be secondary.

I believe in radical transparency. If you are looking for works written entirely by a human hand, I completely respect that, but this story may not be for you. If you are here for the world, the characters, and the care put into crafting the narrative, welcome aboard.
