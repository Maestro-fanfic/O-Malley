Chapter 57 (48 of 54 in this book)

The debrief took three days.

Not because what she'd found required three days to report � she could have given the summary in an hour. The three days were for the full account, the complete version, the kind of record that made the trip intelligible rather than just documented. Haverstock and Fritz and Allison and Carson were all present for different parts of it, each receiving the pieces that were most relevant to their work, and she gave each piece with the full attention of someone who understood that the value of what she'd found was contingent on its being understood.

She told Haverstock about the interface quality at the Hellmouth location, the density of it, the specific character of a site that had been accumulating interface activity for longer than recorded history. He received this with the focused attention of someone who had been working toward this data point for years and was now doing the integration in real time, the theoretical framework meeting the empirical account.

She told Fritz about the transit. The biological experience of moving through the channel at full activation, the specific quality of the lineage running around her, what the Keeper's presence had been like in the passage. He made notes and asked questions that were the precise questions of someone who had been thinking about the theoretical biology of interface transit for years and was now receiving the first firsthand account.

She told Allison about the Girl. The specific quality of the first Slayer's presence during the transit, more present than she'd been since the merge, the sense of the ancient consciousness recognizing the ground it had come from and directing itself toward it with purpose.

Allison said: "The channel and the Girl are the same thing."

She held this.

She said: "Explain."

Allison said: "The modification that produces the channel and the ancient lineage consciousness that presents in the dreams � they're not separate systems. The channel is the biological interface for the lineage's accumulated consciousness. The Girl is what the channel carries." She paused. "When you transit through the interface, you carry both. The Girl's presence intensifying in the transit is the consciousness becoming more accessible as the channel reaches full activation." She paused. "She's always there. The channel is how much of her you can hear."

She sat with this.

She thought about the Girl running through the ancient dark and the dreams and the merge on Hades and the sense of the ancient knowledge available at the edge of everything she did.

She thought: she was always there.

She thought: I've been hearing her for thirteen years.

She said: "She was trying to come home."

Allison said: "Yes. I think so."

She thought about the Girl's directed running in the transit, the purposeful quality, the recognition of the origin ground.

She said: "She found it."

Allison said: "Yes." She paused. "And then you brought her back with you."

She held this.

She thought about what that meant. The first Slayer, who had been created in that place, who had been called from that ground, returning to it for the first time in however many thousands of years�she didn't know when the first Slayer had lived, only that it was before recorded history�and then returning again.

She thought: we both went home. And we both came back.

She thought: that's why it felt like completion.

She said: "Put that in the research." She held Allison's gaze. "The Girl is the channel's passenger and the channel is the Girl's vehicle. That's the mechanism." She paused. "If the lineage network is activating the channel in the subclinical carriers, we're not just opening a biological interface. We're connecting people to the accumulated consciousness of the lineage. To the Girl." She held Allison's gaze. "That's what the resonance quality is measuring."

Allison held her gaze for a long moment.

She said: "That's the next paper."

She said: "That's the next several years of papers."

Allison said: "Yes."

She told Carson about the letter and the data chip.

He held this quietly, the specific quiet of the Clinkscales tradition, the faith kept and the record maintained and the gesture of leaving the knowledge at the origin point.

He said: "The data chip had the archive."

She said: "A copy. Everything current."

He said: "So the record is there. On Old Earth. In the Hellmouth's space."

She said: "If anyone finds it. If the channel reaches it. If�" she stopped. "I don't know what happens with it. But it's there."

He held her gaze.

He said: "The Clinkscales tradition is now on both ends. The record kept here for four generations pointed toward Earth. The record kept on Earth, if anyone kept it�" he paused. "They were pointing toward us."

She held this.

She said: "The lineage is connected. Not just in this network, not just the people we've found. All of it. Across two thousand years and however many light-years." She paused. "The channel doesn't care about distance or time. It's the connection that matters."

He said: "Yes."

She said: "Write that down."

He said: "I've been writing it for four years." He held her gaze. "And I'll write what you just told me now."

She sat back.

She thought about the three days and the debriefs and the accounts and the record being completed and what it felt like to have done what she'd gone to do and come back from it.

She thought: done.

Not the work. The work was never done while it was ongoing.

This. This specific thing. Done.

She thought: yes.

She went home.

~~~

The weeks after had the quality she'd learned to recognize as the settling period, the specific time after significant operations when the body and the mind did the work of integration, the arriving back into the ongoing reality of the life from the elevated reality of the event.

She ran every morning and the running was the running, the body doing what it was built for, not the Girl's ancient running and not the transit's directed running but her own, Buffy Summers's running through the Manticore capital waterfront in the early morning with the wrong-colored harbor water and the Keeper present and the city waking up around her.

She went to Sphinx.

Vigil was in the canopy.

She came onto the property and the bond was full in the immediate way of things that had been at a remove and were suddenly close again, the ambient quality becoming something fuller as she crossed the property's boundary, and she stopped in the garden and looked up at the canopy.

Vigil came down.

All the way down, the ground distance, and it came to her and she held still and Vigil settled against her side and the bond was the bond, complete and present and entirely itself.

She put her arm around the treecat and stood in the garden and breathed.

She said: "I'm back."

The bond's quality was the quality of something that had known she would be and was receiving the being with the fullness of something that hadn't doubted but was glad anyway.

She held Vigil and looked at the garden.

The autumn was past its spectacular peak, into the later stage, the trees beginning to let go of what they'd been holding, and there was something she found beautiful about this stage that she hadn't expected to find beautiful when she'd first arrived on Sphinx. The letting go. The specific generosity of trees that had held everything they had through the growing season and were now releasing it, the leaves coming down not as loss but as the completion of what they'd been building toward.

She stood in the garden in the later autumn and thought about completion.

She thought about the letter on the Hellmouth's floor. She thought about the Girl going home. She thought about what Allison had said, that the channel carried the Girl, that the Girl was always there, that the network's resonance quality was connection to that ancient consciousness.

She thought: I've been carrying her for thirteen years and now she's been home and come back with me and neither of us is the same for it.

She thought: that's the shape of it. That's what completion looks like. Not the end. The going and the coming back.

Vigil made the sound, the specific low note.

She looked down at the treecat.

She said: "Did you know? When you surfaced the grief three years ago. Did you know where it was going to lead?"

The bond's quality was entirely unapologetic.

She thought: of course.

She thought: treecats.

She scratched between Vigil's ears and the autumn came down around them and she was here, in this garden, in this property, in this life, completely.

~~~

She turned thirty-one in January.

Carson came to Manticore for it and Allison made a dinner that was the kind of dinner Allison made when she thought an occasion warranted full effort, which was the kind that required Buffy to eat four portions and still want more. Honor was there and Fritz was there and Yeva came from wherever she'd been operational, which Buffy didn't ask about because Yeva's operational security was Yeva's now and Buffy had learned to stop asking questions whose answers she didn't need.

She sat at the table and looked at the people around it and thought about the table at the Clinkscales house and the dinner the night before the Grayson operation, when she'd been there for the first time and Thomas had come into the kitchen and they'd looked at each other across the room.

She thought about all the tables since.

Allison's table on Beowulf. The Vigil's first formal gathering. The capital quarters table and the Sphinx kitchen and every iteration of the specific human gathering that said you are here with people who are yours.

She was here with people who were hers.

Scotty was beside her.

She looked at him.

He looked at her.

She thought: thirty-one.

She thought: I made it again.

She thought: I intend to keep making it.

Allison said something that made Carson laugh, the specific laugh of someone who is surprised into it, and Honor's expression did the warm layered thing, and Nimitz on Honor's shoulder made the sound, and Vigil who was on the property sent through the bond's ambient quality something that was its version of presence at a distance, the sense of the treecat participating from wherever it was in the Sphinx canopy.

She held all of it.

She thought: this is the table I'm at. This is the life I built. This is what came out of the portal and the Tepes and the jungle and all of it, this specific table on this specific night with these specific people.

She thought: yes. This.

She drank her chocolate and ate Allison's food and listened to Carson and Honor argue about something in the archive's organizational structure that was the specific argument of two people who both cared deeply about the same thing from different professional angles, and she let the evening do what evenings did when they were full.

She thought about what she wanted to say.

She said, when there was a moment: "I want to tell you something."

The table quieted.

She held the quiet.

She said: "I went back to look at where I came from and I came back to here. And I want to say directly, to all of you, that coming back was the easier choice. Not because the other end wasn't real or wasn't worth going to. Because this is where I chose to be." She paused. "I want you to know that. That I chose it."

The table held what she'd said.

Honor said: "We know."

She said: "I wanted to say it anyway."

Allison said: "You should always say the things that want to be said."

She held Allison's gaze.

She said: "I know." She paused. "I've been practicing."

Allison said: "You're getting better at it."

Carson said: "She's been getting better at everything."

She looked at Carson.

He held her gaze with the specific quality of the Clinkscales men when they said something they meant at full weight.

She said: "So have you."

He said: "I had a good example."

She looked around the table.

She said: "So did I."

The table held this and then moved back into its own rhythms, the conversation resuming, Allison redirecting something toward Scotty, Honor countering Carson's archive argument with a point that made him pause and reconsider in the way that meant she'd found the one he hadn't thought of, and the evening continued.

She was thirty-one and at the table and this was the after.

This was, fully and specifically and without reservation, the after.
