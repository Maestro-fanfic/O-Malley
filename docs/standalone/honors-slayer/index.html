Honor's Slayer

a continuation of Honor's Slayer by becuzitswrong

Currently undergoing revision

Buffy the Vampire Slayer x Honor Harrington (David Weber)

Buffy Summers wakes up merged with someone else entirely: a girl from a world with no roads and no language worth the name, on a prison planet called Hades, in the middle of David Weber's Honorverse. What the Watchers' Council trained her for and what the Honorverse actually needs from her turn out to have very little overlap.
