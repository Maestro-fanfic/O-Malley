Chapter 63 (54 of 54 in this book)

Spring came again.

She was in the garden on a morning in early April when the treecat appeared at the edge of the property.

Not Vigil. A different one. She knew immediately and without needing to think about it, the Slayer sense reading the quality of the presence and finding it familiar in the register of the lineage and the channel and what treecats were, and unfamiliar in the specific individual way.

Young. Younger than Vigil had been when Vigil had first come to the property's edge.

It sat at the edge of the canopy and looked at her.

She looked back.

She was sixty-seven and she'd had the bond for twenty-four years and lost it two months ago and the specific quality of the grief was still present and would be present for a long time and was not incompatible with the looking.

She looked.

The young treecat looked.

She thought: not yet. I'm not ready yet.

She stayed where she was.

She said, quietly, to the edge of the canopy: "I see you."

The treecat looked at her.

She said: "I'm not ready yet. But I see you."

She went back to her work in the garden.

The treecat stayed at the edge for a while.

Then it went back up into the canopy.

She worked in the garden and felt the presence at the edge, not the bond, not yet, but something in the vicinity of the bond's channel-frequency, a presence that registered in the Slayer sense in the register that treecats registered.

She thought: when I'm ready.

She thought: it'll be there.

She thought: forward.

The garden was around her and the spring was doing what spring did and she was here, sixty-seven, alive and building and grieving and present, and the work was ahead and the life was here.

She was here.

She thought: still here.

She thought: yes.

She planted what needed planting and the morning moved and the spring held her in its Sphinx specific way and the treecat was somewhere in the canopy and the bond was waiting for when she was ready and the Keeper was present and the document was in Mara's hands and the archive was growing and the Vigil was doing the work and she was here.

She thought: this is what after looks like at sixty-seven.

Not the same as after at thirty or after at fifty-one.

But after.

Still, consistently, always after, and always also the beginning of the next part.

She planted and the spring came in and she was here.

Forward.

She was always, always here.

Forward.
