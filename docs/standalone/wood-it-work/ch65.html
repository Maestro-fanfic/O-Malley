Chapter 65

The days after the audience settled into something Xander hadn't quite let himself hope for: an actual, honest quiet, the kind that didn't feel like it was waiting to be interrupted.

Kestrian's visits turned regular instead of urgent — a standing Tuesday appointment, mostly paperwork, mostly boring, which everyone agreed was the best possible way for a king's herald to spend an afternoon. The oversight arrangement turned out, in practice, to mean a short written report every month and one longer sit-down every season, nothing more dramatic than that, and after the third uneventful report went by without incident, even Willow stopped bracing for it to turn into something worse.

"I keep waiting for the catch," she admitted one evening, filing the latest report into a folder that was, notably, still thin. "Every other time something this big has happened to us, there's been a catch waiting a few weeks out."

"Maybe there isn't one this time," Amy said.

"Maybe," Willow said, unconvinced but willing to let herself hope anyway.

Dara split her weeks evenly now between the shop and the college, comfortable in both places in a way that had taken months to earn and looked, from the outside, like it had always simply been true. Corrin's small classes had grown to four students, then six, careful and slow, each one vetted personally by both her and Alberton before being allowed near the old techniques — "small doors, quiet doors," Corrin liked to say, the same words Meren had apparently used for years before her, "the kind that ask nothing more of the world than to connect one home to one other."

"You've basically become a teacher," Dara told her, one afternoon, watching her correct a student's chalk line with the same patient precision she used for everything.

"I suppose I have," Corrin said, sounding faintly surprised by it even now, months in. "I spent fifteen years certain I'd never trust myself with this again. Turns out trusting myself was never really the problem. I just needed somewhere safe enough to try."
