The Jumper Universe

an unofficial continuation of Steven Gould's Jumper novels

A continuation set directly in Steven Gould's own Jumper universe, following Exo (2014), matching the multi-POV first-person structure Impulse and Exo both use, and slotting into the series' one-word-physics-term naming pattern: Jumper, Reflex, Impulse, Exo, Momentum, Reach, Contact. Chapters are headed by whichever character's POV that chapter belongs to, quoting their own opening line as the chapter's title.

Follows Cent, Millie, and Davy, the next generation of jumpers, as the ability stops being anyone's private secret. A light crossover element runs through Momentum: Dan Truman, NASA's Director of Flight Crew Operations from the film Armageddon (1998), appears as an actual character on the mission-command side of the story, not just an homage.

Entries

  • Momentum

    Jumper (Steven Gould) x Armageddon (1998)

    Currently undergoing revision

    Cent's post-Exo life on Kristen Station is steady, hard-won, almost boring, the good kind. Normal doesn't last. The next generation of jumpers has to find out what all that stability was actually for, with NASA's Dan Truman now in the room for the parts of it that go through official channels.

  • Reach

    Jumper (Steven Gould)

    Currently undergoing revision

    The direct follow-up to Momentum: further out, harder jobs, and a crew that keeps growing past just Cent, Millie, and Davy as jumping stops being anyone's private secret.

  • Contact

    Jumper (Steven Gould)

    Currently undergoing revision

    The trilogy's capstone. A routine orbital cleanup job turns into something that reaches a great deal further than anyone on the team signed up for.
