Chapter 37 of 49

Davy POV: There was no leak. There was just a man who was already in the room.

The President had ordered the answer into his hands before sundown. Baines's team got there with twenty-three minutes left.

Millie and I Jumped to the White House appointment entrance, cleared security, and followed an agent to a secure room in the West Wing. Baines put three things on the table: the attendance roster from the original Cabinet briefings, the authorization trace behind Sterling's revocation order, and a security still of a man standing near the Oval's rear door.

"René Devereaux," she said. "Senior Director, National Security Council staff. He attended both briefings. He used deputy authorities routed through three offices to assemble the revocation order. Each office approved one narrow piece. Devereaux was the only person who knew what the pieces became together."

I read the roster. President. Chief of Staff. National Security Advisor. SecDef. DNI. Truman. Cent. Devereaux among the senior staff and aides whose names never reached the public record.

"There was no leak," I said.

"No," Baines said. "He was cleared for the room. He heard the protection order directly."

Millie picked up the security still. "Cent remembers him. He asked how far the government should let one family control a national capability."

"He's in custody," Baines said. "The President ordered him brought to the Oval for a direct accounting."

The secure phone rang.

Baines answered, listened, and put it on speaker.

"David," the President said.

"I'm here."

"Devereaux was in the room when I gave the protection order. He heard every word. He repeated it back with everyone else."

"Protect, not possess. Partner, not direct. Ask, and accept the answer."

"Exactly that."

"Did anything you authorized give him the right to do what he did?"

"No. He acted directly against my order. He used access I gave him to target your family. There is no version of this where he misunderstood me."

Millie's eyes stayed on Devereaux's face in the security still.

"Millie, I'm so sorry that someone in my administration acted this way! More sorry than I can say," the President said.

"David, I found the motherfucker. You want to come see me rip him to shreds?"

"We're down the hall."

Baines collected the folders. An agent was waiting outside the secure room. He led us down the corridor and opened the Oval Office door.

Devereaux stood near the fireplace with another agent beside him. Halloran and the Chief of Staff waited by the desk. Nobody treated our arrival as a surprise.

The President came in behind me.

"Look at me," he said.
