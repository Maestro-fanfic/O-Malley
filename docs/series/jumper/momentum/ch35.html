Chapter 35 of 49

Holcomb POV: They sent armed men into mission control under my authority?

The Red Phone rang while Halloran was halfway through a sentence about a trade bill.

Sterling's name was on the display.

"Mr. President, there has been an armed attempt to take control of mission control."

I was out of my chair before he finished the sentence.

"They did what?"

"Two men entered on federal credentials carrying a revocation order assembled through national-security channels. They attempted to remove me and replace the command chain while the carrier was in coast. Both men reached for weapons. David Rice and Hunt stopped them. The attackers are alive and in custody. No one else is injured."

"MotherFUCKER! I want this dealt with now!"

Every head within the sound of my voice turned toward me.

The Oval door opened behind Halloran and two Secret Service agents came through it. I waved them off without lowering my voice.

"Nobody leaves that building. Nobody deletes a file, clears a desk, pulls a drive, or carries out so much as a goddamn coffee cup. Lock every credential used in the attempt. Preserve every camera, access log, phone record, and authorization chain. If anyone tells you they outrank that instruction, put them on this line."

"Yes, sir."

"Who signed the order?"

"We don't know yet."

"Find the motherfucker."

"The family?"

"David Rice and Hunt are unhurt. Joe saw the first weapon from his console and Jumped into the corridor."

That stopped me for less than a second.

"Joseph Jumped."

"Yes, sir. Then he checked David Rice, said Cent's name, and disappeared again."

"Where is he?"

"He returned to mission control a few seconds ago. He says he went to Cent, told her everyone was safe, and came back."

Halloran had gone completely still.

"The ship is sixty-seven million kilometers away," I said.

"Yes, sir."

"Did he know where it was?"

"No."

"Ask him to let a doctor examine him. Ask. Do not turn what he just did for his family into an involuntary experiment. Then keep him with David Rice."

"Understood."

"Put Baines in charge of the federal response at the site. FBI and Justice preserve the evidence. Secret Service traces the authority behind the order. Nobody gets exclusive custody of the facts, and nobody involved in issuing that order gets near the investigation. Offer the family protection. Do not block a door, direct a Jump, or pretend protection gives us control of where any of them go."

"Yes, sir."

"And General? The standing order didn't just apply to my house. This family does not get stonewalled at any federal gate while somebody inside uses my name against them."

"It won't happen again."

"See that it doesn't."

I put the Red Phone down hard enough to make the base jump against the table.

"Situation Room," I told Halloran. "Now. I want every person who touched that revocation order, every office it crossed, and the name of whoever decided four minutes of silence made this family available."

"Today. In my hands. Preferably before the sun goes down."

✳

Halloran had barely cleared the door before I picked the phone up again.

"Get me David Rice. Directly."

The connection took less than a minute.

"Mr. President."

"David. The order those men carried did not come from me. They used federal credentials and an authority chain that reached close enough to my office to make the distinction matter, but I did not order anyone to remove Sterling or interfere with your family. The building is locked down, the evidence is being preserved, and I am going to know who did it."

He was silent for a moment.

"I wanted you to hear it from the horse's mouth," I said. "If you'd like, you can come to the Oval and I'll brief you personally."

"I need to finish here first."

"Then finish. The invitation stands."

"Thank you, Mr. President."
