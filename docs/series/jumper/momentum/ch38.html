Chapter 38 of 49

Holcomb POV: You used my order to target her family.

Devereaux was waiting in the Oval when I went back in. An agent stood beside him. Halloran and my Chief of Staff followed me through the door. Baines held the preliminary authorization trace in two open folders. David and Mrs. Rice stood against the wall where the Service had asked them to stand, inside the room and close enough to hear every word.

"Look at me," I said.

He did.

"Nobody in this room is going to answer for you. Nobody is going to explain what you meant. You are going to tell us what you did in plain English."

"Did you order General Sterling's credentials revoked?"

"Sir, the continuity review required..."

"That was not an answer. Did you order it?"

"I authorized a temporary transfer of operational control."

"No. That is language you chose so three offices would sign pieces without seeing the whole. Did you order General Sterling removed from mission control?"

Devereaux looked toward Halloran.

"Do not look at him. Look at me. Did you order it?"

"Yes."

"Who gave you the authority?"

"The continuity provisions allowed..."

"I asked for a name."

He said nothing.

"Who gave you the authority?"

"No one, sir."

"Then say the whole thing. You had no authority, and you sent men carrying guns to enforce your decision."

"I had no authority. I ordered General Sterling removed."

"And sent men carrying guns to do it."

"Their protective authority was standard for..."

"Shut the fuck up."

My voice hit the room hard enough that the agent beside him shifted his stance.

"You waited until Millicent Rice was sixty-seven million kilometers away. You calculated the communications delay. Then you used my protection order to map her family, remove the people she trusted from mission control, and send armed men through the door under my authority."

"Mr. President, her capability cannot remain subordinate to one family's judgment."

"It was never subordinate to yours!"

The door opened. Staff in the outer office had stopped pretending not to listen.

"I told you protect, not possess. I told you partner, not direct. I made every person in this room say it back so no one could hide behind a misunderstanding later. You said it. Then you took my order apart and used the pieces to target her family."

"Both of Millicent Rice's parents are standing in this room because what you did was so far outside my order that they had to hear from me, personally, that their government had not declared war on their family."

Mrs. Rice flickered and was gone.

"FUCK!" Baines shouted.

David came off the wall. The agent beside him caught his arm. David froze rather than risk taking the man through a Jump with him.

"Sir, I acted to preserve national..."

"You do not get to put the word national in front of your own ambition and call it duty."

"And you had no RIGHT to be making that call!"

Mrs. Rice flickered back into place ten feet from Devereaux with a pistol already level in both hands.

Every agent in the room moved.

The agent nearest David drove him back against the wall as two others covered me.

"You tried to use my daughter while she was in danger?"

Bang.

Devereaux went down.

The agent nearest her hit her from the side before the report finished echoing. The pistol struck the carpet and another agent kicked it clear. Two agents covered me with their bodies while a third forced her hands behind her back.

"Hold fire!" I shouted. "She's down."

She did not fight them.

"I'm done," she said.

"Medic," I ordered. "Now."

The agent beside Devereaux checked him, then looked up and shook his head.

Mrs. Rice watched without looking away.

"Mrs. Rice," I said, "you are in federal custody."

"I know."

"You will have counsel."

She nodded once. The agents took her out through the door.

David followed as far as the outer office before two agents stopped him.

"Millie!"

"I'm not hurt," she told him.
