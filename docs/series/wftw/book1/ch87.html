Chapter 87

The symbol research had gone quiet for a stretch, which Faith had learned by now didn't mean nothing was happening, just that Giles was deep in a phase of reading that didn't produce anything worth reporting yet.

"Anything?" she asked, dropping into her usual chair at the Magic Box.

"Slower going than I'd like," Giles admitted, not looking up from his book. "The margin note about jumpers finding one another is the most concrete thing I've found in weeks. Everything else is fragments, half-references, scholars two centuries removed from the actual event making guesses same as we are."

"So basically nothing new."

"Basically nothing new," he agreed. "Though I don't consider that fully discouraging. Some mysteries take far longer than a few months to properly unravel."

"Feels weird, this part being slow. The mural thing moved so fast. Nine days and then it was just — over."

"This is a different kind of mystery. The mural had a deadline forcing our hand. This one doesn't, which means it can afford to be patient, even if that patience is occasionally frustrating."

Faith considered that, turning it over. "Guess I've gotten used to things happening fast around here. Forgot slow's allowed to be its own thing."

"Slow is very often the more honest pace," Giles said. "Fast tends to mean someone's in danger. Slow tends to mean we actually have the luxury of getting it right."

"When you put it that way, slow doesn't sound so bad."

"It rarely does, in retrospect." Giles finally looked up. "How are you finding the pace of things generally? Beyond the research."

"Good, actually. Really good. First time in a while nothing's actively on fire." Faith stretched, working out a knot from patrol the night before. "Feels weird not having a countdown hanging over me. Gonna take some getting used to."

"You're allowed to enjoy the quiet stretches. They don't come around as often as anyone would like in this town."

"Yeah. Trying to actually enjoy it instead of waiting for the other shoe." She glanced at the research table, currently quiet, no urgent countdown ticking beneath it. "Guess I'll let you get back to your fragments and guesses."

"I appreciate the company regardless of the pace," Giles said, and meant it, and Faith left the Magic Box that evening with the strange, unfamiliar comfort of a mystery that could simply wait, and a life around it that, for once, didn't need to rush toward anything at all.

87.5. Chapter 87.5

Willow and Buffy both came this time, the research on Faith's symbol having gone quiet enough that all three of them could afford a few days away without guilt.

The world was savanna again, though wetter than most Xander had visited, a river cutting through the grassland thick with wildlife along both banks.

"Okay, this one's busy," Buffy said, watching a crocodile-adjacent creature slide into the water. "Very busy. Very much a lot going on at once."

"Willow's gonna need a week just for the reptiles," Xander said. "Let alone everything else."

"I've made peace with needing a week for most of these trips at this point," Willow said, already crouched by the water's edge, delighted.

They spent three full days working the riverbank, cataloguing more species in one trip than most worlds offered in a month, and by the final evening had filled nearly every page of Willow's notebook with careful, excited observations.

"This might be the best one yet," Willow said, looking over her notes with visible satisfaction. "Genuinely might be."

"High bar, given everything else we've seen," Buffy said. "But yeah. I'll agree with that assessment."

They made it home exhausted in the good way, cameras full, notebooks bursting, all three of them reminded exactly why this strange, impossible job was worth every complication that came bundled with it.

Faith looked up from the couch as they piled in, still buzzing. "That good, huh? You're all doing the glow thing."

"Best one in a while," Xander confirmed. "You should've come. Genuinely."

"Next time," Faith said, something considering crossing her face again. "Maybe next time."
