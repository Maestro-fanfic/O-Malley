Chapter 124

Buffy came along this time, both of them landing in a world of scattered volcanic islands connected by shallow, warm water, steam rising faintly from distant peaks that looked dormant enough not to worry about.

"Okay, this one's got personality," Buffy said, taking in the steam. "Very moody-landscape energy."

"Moody but hopefully not actively dangerous," Xander said, checking the nearest peak with a practiced eye. "Learned my lesson about active volcanoes a while back."

They spent the first day wading between islands, cataloguing fish in colors bright enough to look almost tropical, and found the actual highlight on the second morning — a colony of crab-like creatures with shells patterned in intricate, almost decorative designs, scuttling across volcanic rock in numbers that turned the whole shoreline into a shifting, clicking mass.

"That's honestly beautiful," Buffy said, crouching to get a closer look. "In a slightly unsettling way. Beautiful-unsettling."

"Willow's gonna lose her whole mind over the shell patterns. I already know it."

"She's gonna want us to catch one, isn't she."

"Absolutely. Already dreading that conversation."

They spent the rest of the trip working the shoreline, careful of loose volcanic rock, and by the third evening had settled into the easy, appreciative quiet that came from a trip asking nothing more of either of them than patience and good footing.

"This is nice," Buffy said, watching steam drift against a darkening sky, leaning back into Xander's chest, his arms coming around her without either of them making a thing of it. "After everything with Natasha and the whole fracture reveal, this is exactly the kind of normal I needed."

"Yeah. Same, honestly. Feels like these quiet ones matter more lately, somehow."

"They do. I think we both know why."

They made it home with crab photos, warm water still clinging to their clothes, glad all over again that the ordinary parts of this life mattered just as much as the extraordinary ones.

Willow met them at the door already reaching. "Please tell me you got photos of whatever it was. I can see it on your faces, you found something."

"Volcanic islands, decorative crabs, zero danger," Buffy confirmed. "Ten out of ten."

"I need every single crab photo immediately."

"Called it," Xander said, already handing over the camera.

Faith was in the kitchen when they came through, quieter than usual, watching the easy way Buffy leaned into Xander while she talked with a look Buffy couldn't quite place — not unkind, just thoughtful in a way that felt, for reasons Buffy didn't examine too closely, like it was about more than just the crab photos.
