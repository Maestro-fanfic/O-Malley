Chapter 13

Millie mentioned the anomaly almost in passing, three weeks before the sedan, during one of her regular calls with Miriam.

"Ilsa's team flagged something odd," Miriam said, mild about it. "A brief unauthorized query against our archive logs, coming from somewhere on your side. Probably a glitch, given how new the infrastructure still is. She wanted it documented anyway."

"Should we worry?" Millie asked, mostly out of thoroughness.

"I don't think so. Small anomalies come up fairly often on an experimental connection like this. I only mention it because we agreed on full transparency, not because it warrants concern."

"I appreciate that. Let us know if anything similar shows up. Better to track a pattern early than dismiss the pieces one at a time."

"Agreed. I'll keep you posted."

Millie told Davy that evening.

"Probably nothing," Davy said. "Glad they told us instead of just quietly looking into it themselves."

"That's the whole point of the alliance." Millie went back to dinner. "I'll mention it to Cent too, given how much time she spends there."

She did, over dinner, and Cent took it with the same mild interest.

"Probably a glitch," Cent agreed. "Connection's still pretty new. Makes sense there'd be hiccups."

Talk moved on — Cent's structural-perception discoveries, the next Serendipity Cliffs trip, ordinary dinner conversation.

None of them thought much more about it in the days that followed.

It would be weeks before any of them understood how much that small anomaly had actually mattered.
