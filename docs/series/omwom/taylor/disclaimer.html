The Legal Part (Because Apparently Terrorists Do Paperwork Too)

Die Hard 2, John McClane, and every catastrophic personnel decision at Dulles belong to 20th Century Studios, Gordon Company, Silver Pictures, Roderick Thorp, Walter Wager, and—after enough corporate acquisitions—the Mouse. Worm, Brockton Bay, and its aggressively traumatized teenagers belong to Wildbow. One property has a studio legal department. The other has several million words and a comment section. I own neither.

This is unpaid fanfiction. No infringement intended. No money made. No hostages exchanged. If Disney, Fox, or Wildbow asks who authorized the crossover, O'Malley did. His office is outside conventional spacetime and does not accept subpoenas.

The passenger-seat premise owes an acknowledged debt to Ack's I, Panacea and Security! This is an homage, not a continuation, and it uses neither Ack's continuity nor his characters. Rob O'Make O'Malley belongs to this sandbox, assuming anyone can prove he exists long enough to serve him papers.

The Restless Clause

O'Make Way, O'Malley! is my sandbox. The shovel is borrowed; the holes are mine.

Under Rule 9a, I, Maestro, grant blanket permission to write additional stories using this sandbox, O'Malley's passenger-seat premise, its core mechanics, and the anthology format in the series bible. Credit the sandbox, disclaim what you do not own, and otherwise have fun. No further conditions apply—except any O'Malley invents after appearing in your protagonist's head, and good luck appealing those.

The Second Opinion Clause

O'Malley may be omnipotent. He is not infallible. Beta readers, canon nitpickers, and anyone willing to explain that a chokepoint is not actually a chokepoint are warmly invited to speak up before publication. He prefers discovering the runway was installed backward before final approach.

Reviews and thoughtful criticism are welcome. Flames will be forwarded to John McClane, who has already crossed broken glass barefoot and is therefore difficult to intimidate through a comment box.
