O'Make Way for Varga

a very meta O'Make, adjacent to the numbered series

An O'Make Way, O'Malley! story

Fandom: Taylor Varga (by mp3.1415player) x O'Make Way, O'Malley! itself

Three girls in one travel slot walk into a theater that already exists inside somebody else's 2.3-million-word fanfic. Mr. O'Malley claims retroactive credit for the whole premise. Nobody asked him to.

✳

The Legal Part (Because Even a 2.3-Million-Word Retcon Needs a Footnote)

Taylor Varga, Taylor, Raptaur, Metis, Ianthe, the door, the workshop, all of it, belongs to mp3.1415player, who has spent years quietly out-writing most professional fantasy epics one update at a time and did not sign off on any of this. Worm, and everyone these three used to be before a Varga-shaped detour got involved, belongs to John "Wildbow" McCrae, whose author bio remains the single most intimidating one in this entire disclosure stack, O'Malley included. I own none of it. Mr. O'Malley owns even less, and mostly just claims the parts he likes.

This is unpaid, unauthorized, deeply affectionate trespassing through someone else's very long story, for the length of one theater scene and no further. It's homage, not continuation: nothing here touches Taylor Varga's own ongoing plot, cast beyond the trio borrowed for a single Gallery cameo, or eventual ending, and none of it happened in mp3.1415player's actual timeline. If the original author would rather this particular door stayed shut, it closes the moment they say so. No Rule 9a required, no argument, no folder deep enough to override it.

The passenger-seat premise carries its usual acknowledged debt to Ack's I, Panacea and Security! Rob O'Make O'Malley belongs to this sandbox, assuming anyone can prove he exists long enough to serve him papers, though given how casually he already claims credit for other people's inciting incidents, good luck finding a process server he'd hold still for.

Whose Sandbox This Is

O'Make Way, O'Malley! belongs to me, not to Ack, and, this one time, not entirely to mp3.1415player either, even though his trio is doing all the actual lifting. Under Rule 9a, I, Maestro, grant blanket permission to write more of it: the ROB O'Make O'Malley mechanic, its rules, the anthology structure, all of it. Credit the sandbox, disclaim what you don't own, have fun. Rule 9a governs my sandbox; it was never mine to extend over someone else's several hundred chapters, so if you want to borrow the Varga trio specifically, that permission slip has a different signature on it, and it isn't mine.

An Invitation, Sincerely Meant

If you know Taylor Varga's actual continuity better than one borrowed cameo can carry, or you've read all four hundred-plus chapters and caught something that doesn't track, say so before this goes further. A fan of the original catching it beats a review catching it.

Reviews and honest reactions are always welcome. Complaints will be received with the same care Metis gives an unindexed dataset: logged, cross-referenced, and quietly held against you forever. Bring your own popcorn; the concessions counter's supply is, per the sign, plot-specific, and this disclaimer does not currently qualify as plot.
