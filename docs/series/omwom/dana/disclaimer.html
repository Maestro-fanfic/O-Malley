Disclaimer & Front Matter

How these stories actually get written lives site-wide on the Please Read This First page, not repeated per story.

The Legal Part (Such As It Is)

Buffy the Vampire Slayer and its characters belong to Mutant Enemy Productions and 20th Television, presently a docile subsidiary of an even larger mouse-shaped empire. Harry, Helen, and Dana Tasker, along with the rest of Omega Sector, belong to 20th Century Fox and James Cameron, who, by pure administrative coincidence, is also currently housed under that same mouse-shaped roof. Both properties therefore share a landlord without sharing so much as a hallway, a state of affairs this story finds more interesting than the studios apparently do.

No infringement is intended and no profit is being made. If anyone from either studio happens to be reading this: it was Loki's idea, he does this sort of thing, ask around.

This is a fan work, offered freely, for love of both properties, an homage to Ack's I, Panacea and Security! and their passenger-seat premise, per the series-wide note above, and to Rob O'Make O'Malley, who does not, as far as anyone can prove, actually exist, and who would very much like it kept that way.

The Restless Clause

O'Make Way, O'Malley! is my own sandbox, not Ack's; the debt to I, Panacea and Security! is one of inspiration and premise, not continuation, and this story is not set in Ack's continuity or written with his characters.

That said, per Rule 9a: I, Maestro, as the creator and sandbox owner of O'Make Way, O'Malley!, give explicit, fully open permission for any author to write additional stories set in this sandbox, the ROB O'Make O'Malley passenger-seat premise, its core mechanics, and the anthology format described in the series bible. No further conditions apply, beyond the standard disclaimer and credit requirements already required of you, and beyond whatever additional terms may be imposed at the sole discretion of Mr. O'Malley, who cannot currently be reached for comment and has historically been unreliable about explaining himself even when he could be.

The Second Opinion Clause

Mr. O'Malley, despite considerable and largely unearned confidence in his own recruiting instincts, would welcome a critical eye on his work. Beta readers, canon nitpickers, and anyone willing to point out a door that doesn't lead where he claimed it would are warmly invited to reach out before a chapter posts, not after. He finds retroactive correction far less charming than most reviewers seem to assume he would.

Reviews and thoughts are genuinely welcome. Flames will be filed, calmly, under Things That Do Not Rattle a Man Who Has Been Shot At By Professionals, and answered with all the urgency Agent Coulson generally reserves for a mall food court.
