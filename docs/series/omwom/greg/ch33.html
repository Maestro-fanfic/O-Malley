Culture Shock

Drey'auc and Rya'c's first full day on Earth began, by mutual and largely unspoken agreement, with breakfast in the commissary, and it was there, watching his son encounter scrambled eggs for the first time in his life, that Teal'c discovered a whole new category of thing he had never once had to prepare himself for.

"This is unnatural," Rya'c announced, prodding the eggs with a fork the way a man might prod something recently deceased. "What creature produces this?"

"A bird, more or less," Daniel offered, delighted, sliding into the seat across from him with his own tray. "Chicken, specifically. It's a whole industry here. You get used to it."

"I do not believe I will get used to it."

::oh man, I actually love this so much, please let me weigh in, this is exactly my area of expertise, being confidently wrong about things...::

"Greg says," Teal'c relayed, straight-faced, "that you will, in fact, get used to it, and that within a month you will be requesting them prepared a specific way that he personally finds excessive."

"There is no excessive way to want eggs," Rya'c said, with the absolute conviction of a teenager who had decided, thirty seconds into an argument, that he had a position worth defending regardless of the actual merits.

"Indeed," Rya'c added, a beat later, in an accidental, unmistakable echo of his father's own cadence that made Daniel choke on his coffee.

"I do not sound like that," Teal'c said.

"You sound exactly like that," Daniel said, still coughing.

Drey'auc adapted faster than her son, watchful and quick, cataloguing the base's rhythms with the same attention she'd once given Apophis's household. She paused at ordinary kindnesses, a held door, an offered chair, still waiting for the catch that never came.

"Nobody here makes kindness a price," she said one evening, more observation than question. "They ask what we know of Apophis. They do not make the answer payment for the room or the food."

"They will ask a great deal," Teal'c said. "Some from curiosity. Some because what you know may keep others alive. You may answer in your own time."

"That is a strange kind of relief. Being wanted for the wrong reasons instead of the right ones."

✳

The television, inevitably, became the flashpoint. Rya'c discovered Teal'c's westerns within the first week and took to them with an enthusiasm that startled everyone, arguing plot points with the same fierce, absolute confidence he'd brought to the eggs, and it was watching father and son debate whether a particular sheriff should have trusted a particular deputy that Teal'c understood, more clearly than any single conversation could have told him, that something in his family had actually, genuinely begun to heal rather than merely survive.

::this is what you missed. Four years of this. I'm sorry you missed it.::

::I am here for it now,:: Teal'c said. ::That will have to be enough, because it is the only version available to either of us.::

::yeah. I think it's actually enough, though. I really think it is.::

Drey'auc watched the two of them argue with the same quiet contentment Teal'c had come to recognize as her own version of joy, unshowy, careful, hard-won, and did not interrupt even once, letting the argument run its course exactly as long as it needed to.
