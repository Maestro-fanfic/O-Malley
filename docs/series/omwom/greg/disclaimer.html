For the Record (Legal Notice)

Stargate SG-1 is the property of MGM Television and whichever corporate descendant currently holds the deed, born out of the 1994 film by Dean Devlin and Roland Emmerich and carried into a series by Brad Wright and Jonathan Glassner. Worm belongs entirely to John "Wildbow" McCrae, who wrote several million words of it unassisted, which remains the single most intimidating author bio anywhere in this disclosure. No gate address currently connects the two properties, and any resemblance between how they're run is coincidence, though Stargate Command would probably want it classified anyway.

No profit changes hands here, and no infringement is intended by any of it. Should a representative of MGM or Mr. McCrae happen across this: notify General Hammond, he'll want the paperwork on his desk before this goes any further.

This is a fan work, offered freely, for love of both properties, an homage to Ack's I, Panacea and Security! and their passenger-seat premise, per the series-wide note above, and to Rob O'Make O'Malley, who does not, as far as anyone can prove, actually exist, and who would very much like it kept that way.

Whose Sandbox This Is

O'Make Way, O'Malley! belongs to me, not to Ack. Whatever this premise owes I, Panacea and Security! is a debt of inspiration, not shared continuity; nothing here is set inside Ack's own version of events, and none of his characters appear.

Rule 9a still applies. I, Maestro, creator and sole proprietor of this sandbox, give open, standing permission for anyone who wants to write more of it, the ROB O'Make O'Malley mechanic, its rules, the anthology structure, all of it, to go ahead. The only conditions are the ordinary ones: proper credit, the standard disclaimer, and whatever Mr. O'Malley himself might decide to add unilaterally, a man who has never once been reachable for comment and shows no sign of starting now.

An Invitation, Sincerely Meant

For all his confidence in his own eye for a good match, Mr. O'Malley would genuinely welcome somebody checking his work. If you know Stargate lore, Worm lore, or just good storytelling, and something doesn't sit right, say so before a chapter goes up, not after. Corrections land a great deal better ahead of the fact than behind it.

Reviews and honest reactions are always welcome. Complaints will be received with all the patience Teal'c generally reserves for a recruit who hasn't yet learned not to point a weapon at his own foot, filed calmly, and not dwelled on further.
