Reyes Finds a Lead

"Time you have is time the enemy doesn't." — Murphy's Laws of Combat

Wednesday, September 23, 1998; the Threshold, Crawford Street Manor, Sunnydale; Evening 7:00pm —

"Got something," Reyes said, sliding into the booth across from Michaela and Xander, a folder thicker than his usual reports. "Took some digging, and I had to call in favors I was saving for something else, but there's a salvage operation up in the Pacific Northwest — private collector, real recluse, sits on tech nobody else even knows exists because he's never once tried to sell any of it. Word is he's got at least one intact power core matching Cassidy's spec, pulled off a wreck decades back that nobody's ever properly identified."

"He's willing to sell?"

"That's the complication." Reyes's expression turned careful. "He's not really a seller. He's a collector, the genuine obsessive kind — the pleasure's in having the thing, not profiting off it. Money's not gonna move him. I think this needs a different kind of approach. Somebody who can actually talk to him about what the core would be used for, make him understand it's not just another trophy somebody's chasing."

Xander was already pulling out his own notes, logistics brain engaging. "So we need somebody who can make the personal case. Not a transaction. A genuine appeal."

"That's my read," Reyes said. "I can get you the introduction. I can't make the actual case for you. That part's gotta come from somebody he'd actually listen to."

Michaela considered this. "I think," she said slowly, "that's Cassidy's own case to make, if she's willing. Nobody could make it more than the person it would actually save."

"Would she be up for the trip?" Xander asked.

"I don't know," Michaela said. "But I think we ask her, rather than deciding for her. She's earned that much, at minimum."
