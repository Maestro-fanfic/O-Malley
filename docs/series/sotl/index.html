Ship of the Line

an anthology built on Zaion's Halloween-costume challenge

Built on Zaion's original "Ship of the Line" challenge: a Halloween costume becomes a permanent identity merger, not just a power. Each entry answers that challenge differently, its own crossover pulled in alongside the costume-shop premise, its own cast, its own shape for what the costume actually becomes.

Entries

  • Ship of the Line - Convergence

    Buffy the Vampire Slayer x Stargate

    Complete (59 chapters)

    On Halloween, Janus opens a doorway instead of creating an illusion. For a few hours, Xander Harris becomes the bridge to Eli Wallace aboard the real Destiny—and Eli recognizes Earth outside the window. What begins with waking Colonel Young and contacting Stargate Command forces two governments, the Watchers Council, and a god of thresholds to reckon with what comes next.

  • New Guard

    Buffy the Vampire Slayer x Superman/DC x Stargate SG-1 x The West Wing x Star Trek

    Complete (24 chapters, numbered 14–37)

    Future Xander sent Ethan Rayne back with warning of a Goa'uld invasion and a desperate plan: make one Halloween transformation permanent. The world now has a young Superman, a crashed Enterprise, and only months to turn foreknowledge into a defense that does not depend on one teenager saving everyone alone.

  • Ship of the Line: The City Who Fought (working title)

    Buffy the Vampire Slayer x The City Who Fought (Anne McCaffrey & S.M. Stirling)

    In development

    A third answer to Zaion's “Ship of the Line” challenge. Not yet started; further details to come.
