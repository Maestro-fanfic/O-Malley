Chapter 46 of 59

False Intelligence

The best lies in Limen's report were facts moved slightly out of place.

Destiny was damaged.

Its crew was divided between military and civilian authority.

Most of the expedition remained in stasis.

The ship recognized access patterns inherited by Alexander Harris.

Its path back to Earth terminated beneath Sunnydale High.

All of those statements were true.

Selmak, Carter, Daniel, Young, Wray, and O'Neill constructed the deception around them.

The report claimed Destiny's solar recharge had restored only enough power for one return journey. It described the collectors as failing and the weapons grid as mostly inaccessible. It presented Young and Rush as rival commanders, Hammond as an Earth general struggling to control either one, and the awakened crew as close to mutiny over contact with their families.

It described Xander as the only person capable of activating Destiny's critical systems.

It described the Sunnydale mooring as a chain that would drag the ship back if it attempted to flee.

“Too much,” Young said.

Carter looked up from the draft. “Which part?”

“Making Xander the only operator. The scout observed multiple engine maneuvers. Janus will assume trained crew.”

Rush appeared on another screen from Destiny.

“Then say Harris is required for navigation and access to restricted Ancient systems. It is more credible and substantially more attractive.”

Xander sat at the end of the conference table.

“Nice to feel substantially attractive.”

Nobody laughed.

Wray pushed the report aside.

“We are discussing naming a seventeen-year-old civilian as the single point of failure in Earth's defense. Before that becomes a drafting question, he receives the full plan.”

Hammond agreed.

The room was cleared of personnel not required for the decision. Buffy remained. So did Giles, at Xander's request. Young stayed on the channel. Saroosh and Selmak represented the Tok'ra route through which the message would travel.

O'Neill explained what naming Xander would do.

The false Janus already knew his name and knew that Destiny responded to him. The report would confirm that he mattered, exaggerate how much, and encourage capture rather than bombardment. It would also make Xander a priority target for every Jaffa in the approaching force.

The protections were specific.

Xander's home and school routine would be replaced by controlled movement until the threat ended. His parents would be evacuated under a cover story without being told his role. A protective team would remain near him without commanding him. No operation would use his live location without his approval. If he chose to remain aboard Destiny, Young would control physical access to him. If he chose the ground, Buffy and O'Neill would control the defense.

The plan would be abandoned if the false report created a threat the SGC could not mitigate.

“Can I change my mind after the message goes?” Xander asked.

Hammond answered. “You can withdraw from any active role. We cannot make the enemy forget your name.”

“So the risk doesn't go away.”

“No.”

“What happens if I say no now?”

“We remove the claim concerning you and construct the deception around the ship's damaged power systems.”

Rush looked displeased.

Young did not.

Buffy addressed Xander, not Hammond.

“You don't have to do this because you're already involved.”

“I know.”

“Or because Eli would.”

“I know.”

“Or because they'll call it saving the world.”

Xander looked at her.

“You really hate that one.”

“Experienced consumer.”

He read the report again.

Limen had already given Janus his name. Removing it now would not make him safe. It would only leave the enemy to guess why he mattered. The lie could shape the guess toward capture and toward a battlefield Earth chose.

“Use my name,” Xander said. “Not my parents. No home address. No claim that I'm under military orders or that taking me gives anybody legal control of the ship.”

Daniel revised the language.

“And I approve the final version before it goes.”

Wray added the condition.

Hammond looked at Buffy. “The report also identifies the Hellmouth as the ship's ground anchor. That places Sunnydale under direct threat.”

“It already is.”

“This increases it.”

“Then I want the actual ground-defense plan before you send anything.”

“Agreed.”

Consent did not remove the danger.

It decided who was permitted to accept it.

✳

The final report used Limen's encryption and stylistic markers taken from seven years of archived transmissions.

Selmak reproduced the operative's formal address to Janus. Daniel inserted the cult language expected from a loyal servant. Carter degraded the technical data precisely enough to resemble a hurried report assembled while under surveillance.

Xander removed one sentence claiming he could command Destiny by thought.

“I can't, and if they build a plan around that, they'll notice the first time they point a brain scanner at me.”

Rush objected to another sentence describing the primary weapon network as inoperable.

“An enemy who believes that will not account for our known engagement with the scout.”

They changed inoperable to unstable after limited discharge.

True enough to explain the observed fight.

False enough to invite another.

Buffy reviewed the ground claims. Young reviewed the ship claims. Xander approved the use of his name.

Hammond authorized transmission.

Carter activated Limen's crystal.

The false report left Earth through a route the Goa'uld believed secure.

It described a wounded Ancient prize, a divided crew, a vulnerable doorway, and a human key.

Then Earth waited to see whether a false god would believe what he wanted to be true.
