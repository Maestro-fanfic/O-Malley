Chapter 59 of 59

Home

Three weeks after the battle, Neris opened her eyes alone.

Cardea was gone.

The extraction had lasted nine hours. Neris survived two seizures, cardiac arrest, and injuries the Goa'uld inflicted while being removed. Selmak remained with her through all of it. Fraiser slept for fourteen hours afterward and returned before her patient woke.

Neris asked the date.

Then she asked whether the ship had been captured.

O'Neill told her it had.

“By whom?”

“Complicated coalition. Mostly people who don't agree about anything else.”

Neris considered that.

“Then Cardea would have hated it.”

Her voice was weak.

It was entirely her own.

Anne Mercer survived Limen's removal two days later. Recovery would take longer than captivity had taught her to expect. Basil arranged for her Council testimony to be taken only when she chose and with independent counsel present. No one treated the crimes committed through her body as hers.

The hosts were not restored to the people they had been before.

They were alive to decide who came next.

✳

The President placed the settlement under Hammond.

The written authority was narrow enough to be accountable and broad enough to function. Hammond commanded the joint Earth response to Destiny, the Stargate, and immediate extraterrestrial threats. He reported to the President. Allied governments received defined participation where their citizens, territory, or institutions were involved.

Young retained operational command of Destiny.

He remained a United States Air Force colonel rather than declaring himself captain of an independent future state. His orders came through Hammond. His responsibility for his ship and crew remained explicit within them.

The awakened expedition began building lives in a year many of them remembered only as childhood.

Some remained aboard. Some accepted secure quarters on Earth. No one contacted a younger self or family without review, preparation, and the consent of every person who could reasonably be consulted. Psychological care was treated as mission support rather than weakness.

The sleeping crew would wake gradually.

Medical need, ship function, representation, and individual circumstances determined the order. Convenience did not. Each person received a briefing before seeing Earth. Each received a choice about service after immediate safety permitted it.

Rush called the process inefficient.

Wray placed him on the review board.

He stopped using the word where she could hear it.

The Tok'ra agreement ceased to be provisional after both sides ratified revised terms. It remained limited. Either side could withdraw. Intelligence exchanges were logged. Host protections were mandatory. Access to Destiny required purpose and permission.

Trust did not become unconditional.

It became durable enough to survive disagreement.

✳

Destiny left Earth once during the third week.

Young ordered a short course beyond the Moon, held position for an hour, and returned through the transformed coordinate.

The ship resumed station above Sunnydale by choice.

The mooring held.

It was not a chain.

✳

The Watchers Council did not survive in its former shape.

Basil delivered the report to the Queen in person.

Council authority over Slayers and Potentials remained suspended. Its records were secured in multiple protected repositories. Criminal investigations continued into homicide, conspiracy, child exploitation, unlawful imprisonment, evidence destruction, and every death concealed beneath the word retirement.

Individual Watchers received individual judgment.

Several had risked their positions to protect girls assigned to them. Their evidence helped reconstruct missing lives. Others had signed orders, administered drugs, arranged tests, or looked away. Tradition excused none of them and condemned none without proof.

“And Miss Summers?” the Queen asked.

“She has accepted the support agreement, Ma'am.”

“Accepted?”

“After revising it extensively.”

The Queen almost smiled.

Buffy accepted retroactive compensation, medical care, education support, equipment, survivor and disability protection, and operational funding for the team she chose. None was conditional upon obedience to the Council, military service, or continued patrol.

She remained the Slayer.

She also remained Buffy Summers.

“Mr. Giles?”

“Remains her Watcher by her invitation.”

The invitation had taken place in the damaged library.

Giles stood across from Buffy while construction crews measured the wall behind him.

“I don't know if I forgive you yet,” she said.

“I understand.”

“You don't get Council orders anymore.”

“No.”

“You don't make decisions about my body. No tests. No drugs. No secrets you call necessary because somebody in England wrote them down.”

“Agreed.”

“And if I ask whether you knew something, you answer the question I asked.”

Giles looked at her without the protection of his glasses.

“Yes.”

Buffy let the silence remain.

“Then I want you to stay.”

Giles closed his eyes.

“As my Watcher,” she continued. “Because I choose you. Not because they did.”

“I shall endeavor to deserve the distinction.”

“Good. Because deserving is ongoing.”

“I have recently been instructed on that point.”

The Scoobies entered the official record as the operational support structure that had made Buffy the most successful Slayer in the surviving history.

Willow received research access, training, and rules intended to keep magical study from consuming the person conducting it. Giles received a salary no longer tied to Council obedience. Xander received compensation for prior patrol support separately from his agreement with Destiny.

None became employees required to fight.

Their friendship remained the part no institution could manufacture.

The Queen opened the next Slayer file.

Her name was Asha Devi.

She had been called at fourteen and died at fifteen. Her Watcher requested medical support twice. Both requests had been denied as unnecessary expense.

The Queen read every page.

Then she opened the next.

✳

Xander returned to school in a building borrowed from the community college.

His schedule included algebra, English, history, two protected liaison periods, and medical appointments with TJ that did not become optional because he felt fine. Destiny recognized his access under his own name. The Air Force recognized that a civilian badge was not an invitation to issue orders.

Principal Snyder recognized none of those distinctions and objected to the liaison periods until the Department of Education sent a representative with federal credentials and no sense of humor.

Willow found this deeply satisfying.

Buffy began patrol after sunset.

She carried a secure radio. Giles and Willow monitored from the temporary library. A medical team remained available without following her down every alley. O'Neill's people maintained a threat channel and stayed out of her way unless called.

Support did not make the night safe.

It meant someone was obligated to notice whether she came home.

She checked the radio once.

“Patrol beginning.”

“Copy,” Giles answered.

Buffy looked at Willow.

“Still weird.”

“Good weird?”

Buffy considered the question.

“Ask me when we get back.”

They walked into the dark together.

✳

At Cheyenne Mountain, Hammond watched Destiny hold its impossible station over Sunnydale.

It no longer appeared on civilian instruments. Its position remained physically absurd, politically difficult, and strategically indispensable.

It was also full of people who had come home before leaving.

Hammond signed the day's orders and turned off the display.

✳

Young found Xander on the observation deck.

Earth filled the window as it had on Halloween night. California turned beneath them. Sunnydale lay invisible at this distance, marked only by the coordinate stored inside the ship.

A simple memorial stood beside the glass.

It carried one name.

ELI WALLACE

Beneath it, the ship's record listed his final actions.

He recognized Earth.

He woke Colonel Everett Young.

He established contact with Stargate Command.

He transmitted Destiny's condition and position.

He transferred his system work and command access.

He identified Alexander Harris and stated that what happened was not Xander's fault.

He brought Destiny home.

Young stood beside Xander.

Neither spoke for a while.

Below them, Buffy patrolled with friends beside her, a radio at her shoulder, medical help available, and governments newly obligated to care whether she returned.

At Buckingham Palace, the Queen opened another file and read another girl's name.

At the SGC, Hammond's night watch tracked Destiny's steady signal.

Xander touched Eli's old identification badge inside his sweatshirt.

“He got you home,” Xander said.

Young looked down at Earth. “He got all of us home.”

“I'm not him.”

“No.” Young turned to him. “You're the reason he had a door.”

Xander looked through Eli's window as himself.

Janus opened the door. Eli crossed the threshold. Everyone else chose what the new beginning would become.
