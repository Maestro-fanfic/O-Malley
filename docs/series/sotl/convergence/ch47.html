Chapter 47 of 59

The Agreement

The provisional alliance required eleven drafts.

The first failed because the Tok'ra used access where Hammond required specific requested access under supervision.

The third failed because the American draft described hosts and symbiotes together as Tok'ra personnel without explicitly preserving the host's independent personhood.

The fifth failed when the Tok'ra reserved the right to destroy captured operatives who might reveal resistance cells.

Buffy read that paragraph aloud.

“No.”

Garshaw attended the negotiation through the Stargate link. “A captured Goa'uld may possess the location of thousands of Tok'ra.”

“Then you secure the information. You don't execute the person the Goa'uld is wearing.”

“The choice may not always be available.”

“Then the agreement says protection whenever possible and requires somebody to prove when it wasn't.”

Wray translated the demand into language both sides could enforce.

The seventh draft failed because the Tok'ra described Destiny as a jointly relevant strategic resource.

Young removed the phrase.

“Destiny is my ship and my crew.”

Garshaw's image regarded him across the briefing room.

“It is an Ancient vessel whose existence affects every power in this galaxy.”

“That makes it strategically relevant. It doesn't make it yours.”

Hammond supported Young.

Destiny remained under Young's operational command and Hammond's lawful authority. Tok'ra assistance created no claim of ownership, command, salvage, or unrestricted inspection. Access would require a defined purpose, time, location, and approval from both commanders.

The ninth draft failed because it called Buffy and Xander protected persons.

“We are,” Xander said.

“We are also participating,” Buffy answered. “Protection doesn't mean we become luggage.”

The final language recognized them as civilian partners entitled to protection, information relevant to their decisions, and the right to refuse roles not freely accepted. Neither could be transferred, assigned, or offered as part of any military or diplomatic arrangement.

The phrase seemed absurd until everyone remembered how often powerful institutions described people as resources.

The eleventh draft held.

Earth would share selected intelligence from captured Goa'uld systems and limited technical observations relevant to the common threat. The Tok'ra would provide current warnings, relay access, Goa'uld technical assistance, and aid in protecting and recovering hosts. Neither side would conduct operations in the other's territory without permission except to prevent an immediate and otherwise unavoidable loss of life.

Information received through the alliance could not be used to coerce blending, recruitment, military service, or technological access.

Jaffa who surrendered would be treated as prisoners capable of choosing a different allegiance, not as disposable extensions of their masters.

The agreement did not require trust.

It created procedures through which trust might become rational.

The signing took place in the SGC briefing room.

Hammond signed for the United States under presidential authority. Basil witnessed the terms for the British government and the Crown where the Council and Sunnydale matters intersected. Young signed the command annex governing Destiny.

Garshaw signed for the Tok'ra resistance.

Saroosh took the pen.

She wrote her own name first.

Saroosh.

Her eyes glowed. The grip changed subtly in the same hand.

Beneath the first signature, Selmak wrote a second.

Selmak.

Two names. One body. Neither concealed inside the other.

Buffy and Xander did not sign the alliance as governments or soldiers. They signed acknowledgments defining the roles they had accepted and the protections no party could trade away.

O'Neill signed as operational witness.

Wray closed the final document.

“Effective upon exchange.”

Garshaw inclined her head.

“Then we are allied.”

“Provisionally,” Hammond said.

Saroosh smiled.

“All enduring agreements begin provisionally.”

The alert from the Tok'ra listening post arrived before the ink dried.

Janus had answered Limen.
