Chapter 53 of 59

The Ground Assault

The Goa'uld transports appeared over Sunnydale in daylight.

Their cloaks failed in sequence as Carter's interference transmitters found the frequencies recovered from the first transport. Three dark shapes emerged above the town, each descending toward a separate landing zone. Death gliders crossed ahead of them and fired on roads, substations, and communications towers.

The town should have panicked.

Most of it was already gone.

Buses had moved residents under the chemical-emergency cover. Police closed the remaining streets. Hospitals transferred critical patients. The people who could not be told the truth had still been given the chance to leave it behind.

Buffy watched the first transport land from the school roof.

“East field,” she reported. “Exactly where Teal'c predicted.”

The Air Force lieutenant colonel beside her relayed the position to conventional teams. Giles marked it on the library map. Willow tracked the mooring from the basement. Basil coordinated the last civilian clearances.

Nobody waited for Buffy to do all of it.

She concentrated on the part only she could see.

The Hellmouth was awake.

Fear moved through Sunnydale like weather. Vampires rose in dark buildings hours before sunset. Demons emerged from the sewer network and followed the pressure toward the school. The approaching Jaffa registered to them as violence, blood, and an opening boundary.

“Movement under sector four,” Buffy said.

The conventional commander checked his sensors. “Nothing showing.”

“That's the problem. Pull the team off the storm drain.”

He gave the order.

Thirty seconds later, something broke the grate from below.

It had six legs, a human mouth, and no interest in which planet its prey came from. Staff fire struck it from one direction. Air Force rifles struck it from another. The creature kept moving until Buffy dropped from the roof and put a sword through the soft joint beneath its jaw.

“Mixed threat,” she said into the command channel. “If it isn't wearing gold armor, don't assume it's friendly.”

The first Jaffa formation advanced behind portable shields.

Bullets flattened against gold light. Staff blasts tore apart the barricades from outside rifle range. The conventional line fell back according to plan, drawing the formation between two buildings where the shield could not widen.

Buffy's inner team hit from above.

Crossbow bolts did little against armor. Blessed water did nothing at all. A weighted cable dropped between the Jaffa and pulled three staff weapons out of firing alignment. Buffy landed inside the shield with two SGC soldiers and one of the surrendered Jaffa acting as adviser.

“Joint below the arm,” he shouted.

Buffy already knew.

She struck, turned, and sent the warrior into his comrade. Zat fire followed her opening. The portable shield collapsed when its bearer fell.

The conventional teams resumed fire.

The formation withdrew in order.

“They're learning,” the lieutenant colonel said.

“So are we.”

The second transport landed beside Restfield Cemetery.

That was where the plan began to fail.

The Jaffa advanced through the graves in disciplined lines. Staff blasts opened mausoleums and shattered crypt doors. The dead inside did not appreciate being shelled.

Vampires came out behind the Jaffa.

The Air Force team watching the western road saw enemy soldiers and shifted to engage them. It did not see the shapes moving through the smoke at its back.

Buffy heard the first scream over the radio.

“Western team, turn around!”

Gunfire changed direction. A vampire reached the radio operator before he could bring up his weapon. Teal'c's volunteer adviser staked it with a piece of shattered fence and looked surprised when it became dust.

More rose.

The Jaffa mistook them for an Earth defense until the vampires attacked both sides.

The cemetery became a three-way battle.

Conventional training alone could not distinguish a wounded civilian from a vampire waiting for invitation range. Supernatural experience alone could not cross open ground under coordinated staff fire. The defense worked only when both groups stopped treating the other's knowledge as an accessory.

Giles called vulnerabilities over the command net.

SGC teams marked buildings cleared of civilians.

Buffy shifted reserves between threats before either could turn a flank.

Medical teams received the living. Separate containment teams watched the dead.

At the school, Willow felt something touch the mooring from above.

“They're scanning us.”

Giles looked at the three keys inside the protected circle.

One had begun to vibrate.

“Can they affect it?”

“Not yet.”

The basement ceiling shook.

Dust fell across the circle.

An Al'kesh passed over the school and fired on the outer defensive line. The blast opened a crater in the football field and exposed utility tunnels leading toward the foundation.

Jaffa entered the crater carrying naquadah explosives.

Buffy saw their movement on the Kino feed Xander routed from Destiny.

“They found the lower route.”

“Inner reserve ready,” the conventional commander said.

Buffy looked at the cemetery, the eastern line, and the creatures moving beneath the streets.

Every position needed her.

For the first time, that did not mean she had to occupy all of them.

“Send the reserve to the tunnels. Giles, begin reinforcement. Western team follows its adviser on vampire calls. East holds without pursuit.”

“And you?” O'Neill asked over the distant channel from Cardea's ship.

Buffy lifted her sword.

The third transport was descending directly toward the school.

“I have the front door.”
