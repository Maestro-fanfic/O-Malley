Chapter 37 of 59

The Battle Below

The first Kino found the Jaffa commander beneath the football field.

Xander guided the small silver sphere through a storm drain from Destiny's control room. The ship had returned from Jupiter carrying the disabled scout against its lower hull. Its orbit once again centered above Sunnydale, and the transformed anchor carried control signals to the ground with less delay than physics permitted.

The Kino rose through a grate and looked into a maintenance tunnel.

Nine Jaffa moved below the school grounds. Two carried cases large enough to contain heavy weapons. A third carried a cylindrical device marked with the Goa'uld symbols for opening, cleansing, and final passage.

Xander magnified it.

Teal'c saw the image at the school command post.

“A naquadah charge.”

O'Neill stopped beside him. “Yield?”

“Sufficient to destroy the school and open the Hellmouth beneath it.”

Giles looked toward the basement floor.

“Open it how far?”

“Farther than your ritual did.”

“Then we shall avoid that.”

Buffy studied the tunnel map on the library table. The Jaffa had entered through a storm culvert east of the field. Their route would bring them beneath the science wing and down to the foundation surrounding the Hellmouth chamber.

“Where are the rest?” she asked.

Xander divided the Kino feeds. “Five at the cannery extraction point. Four moving down Revello. Three watching the transport rings. I lost two in the warehouse district.”

He reported positions, direction, and equipment. He did not tell Buffy where to place anyone.

That decision remained hers.

“The bomb team is the threat,” she said. “The others are there to keep us busy. We stop the ones under the school and block the transport rings so they can't pull out.”

O'Neill pointed to the science-wing access. “I can put a containment team here.”

“They'll shoot through the wall.”

“Staff weapons?”

“No. Chemistry classroom. Half those cabinets are one bad sneeze away from becoming mustard gas.”

Giles stared at her.

“I'm exaggerating.”

Willow raised a hand. “Not by as much as you'd hope.”

Buffy moved the containment team to the old boiler corridor. She sent Teal'c and two zat teams to the storm culvert behind the Jaffa. O'Neill took the basement approach. Giles and Willow remained at the anchor with guards and instructions to evacuate through the library stairs if the inner line failed.

Buffy entered the tunnel alone.

Not because she lacked support.

Because the first contact needed someone fast enough to survive it.

✳

The Kino drifted ahead of her, close to the ceiling.

“Thirty meters,” Xander said through her earpiece. “They stopped.”

Buffy stopped too.

“Did they see the Kino?”

“No. Commander is checking a detector.”

“For me?”

“For the anchor. You're between them and it.”

“Story of my life.”

The Jaffa detector chirped.

The commander raised his staff weapon.

“Down!” Xander shouted.

Buffy dropped as plasma crossed the tunnel and destroyed the Kino behind her. The blast filled the culvert with white light and fragments of concrete.

She ran into the smoke.

The lead Jaffa expected an army. Buffy came under his weapon, struck the joint beneath his breastplate, and used his falling body to block the next shot. She seized the staff, opened its head by accident, and drove it backward into the tunnel wall before it discharged.

The explosion separated the commander from the charge team.

“Now!” Buffy called.

O'Neill's containment team opened the boiler-room access ahead. Teal'c's team entered through the culvert behind. Zat fire crossed above Buffy as she rolled beneath a staff strike.

The Jaffa formation contracted around the naquadah charge.

They did not break.

Their commander activated his helmet.

“Warriors of Janus, the final gate opens!”

He reached for the charge.

Buffy threw the captured staff weapon.

It struck his wrist and pinned his arm against the case. Teal'c fired a zat blast into the warrior guarding the detonator. O'Neill shot the device from his hand before he could fall on it.

The charge rolled across the tunnel.

Everyone froze except Buffy.

She caught it before it struck the wall.

“Heavy,” she said.

O'Neill exhaled. “Please don't juggle the alien bomb.”

“Wasn't planning to.”

The commander tore his arm free. Blood ran from beneath the armor. He drew a short blade and attacked Buffy with no concern for the weapons trained on him.

His first strike was disciplined.

His second was furious.

By the fourth, he was no longer fighting Buffy. He was fighting the fact that his god's scout had fallen, that an Ancient vessel had passed beyond Janus's sight, and that the threshold he had come to claim belonged to people who refused to kneel.

Buffy let him spend the anger.

Then she stepped inside his guard, broke the fastening beneath his shoulder, and tore the heavy armor sideways. His balance followed it. She struck once at the throat joint and once behind the knee.

The commander hit the floor.

Buffy took his blade.

“Yield.”

His eyes burned with hatred.

“Janus closes every road before his enemies.”

“Then it's lucky we're not on his road.”

He reached for a hidden weapon.

Buffy broke his wrist and put the blade against his throat.

She did not cut it.

Around them, the surviving Jaffa waited for an order from a commander who could no longer give one.

Teal'c lowered his staff weapon first.

“The vessel which brought you is captured,” he said. “Your transport cannot escape Destiny. Your charge is secured. Your commander is defeated.”

One Jaffa looked at the naquadah case in Buffy's hands.

“The Tau'ri will kill us.”

“No,” Buffy said.

The commander laughed from the floor. “They will place you in chains and call the chain mercy.”

Buffy looked at the restraints around his wrists.

“You came here to kidnap my friend and blow up my school. You're not getting a parade.”

She faced the others.

“Put down the weapons. You get medical treatment, food, and a chance to decide what you believe when he isn't deciding it for you.”

The oldest Jaffa studied Teal'c's exposed mark.

“You offer us the path of a shol'va.”

“I offer you a choice,” Teal'c said.

The first staff weapon struck the floor.

Then the second.

The sound traveled through the tunnel one weapon at a time.

Above ground, the remaining Jaffa received the surrender through their own communications. Some fled toward the transport rings. The SGC blocked them without firing. Others held position until Teal'c spoke to them directly.

By dawn, twenty surviving Jaffa were in custody. The wounded received treatment in the school gymnasium beside the people they had injured.

The Goa'uld transport remained cloaked above the cannery until Carter used the captured scout's codes to order it visible.

It appeared against the morning sky like a blade emerging from clear water.

Sunnydale had acquired another impossible object.

This one belonged to Earth by right of failed invasion.
