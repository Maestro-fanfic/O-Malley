Chapter 56 of 59

The Line Holds

The first naquadah charge detonated beneath the football field.

The Jaffa had not reached the mooring. They did not need to. The blast collapsed two utility tunnels, broke the school's water main, and drove a fracture through the concrete surrounding the Hellmouth chamber.

The protected circle jumped six inches sideways.

One iron key left its mark.

Willow caught it before it struck the broken floor.

The mooring changed pitch.

Nobody heard the sound through the air. Giles felt it in his teeth. Xander heard it aboard Destiny as every return coordinate divided into two possible locations.

“Ground point destabilizing,” he reported.

Young looked from the tactical display to Earth.

“Can you hold it?”

Willow answered through the basement channel.

“Ask me after I do.”

She pushed the key back into place.

Giles began the reinforcement invocation.

Above them, Buffy fought for three minutes.

That was the contingency. Three uninterrupted minutes to collapse the surface connection or restore it. The inner reserve formed around the basement approaches. Conventional troops held the stairs. Giles's trained teams guarded the vents and tunnels against creatures the Air Force could not identify in time.

Buffy moved between all of them.

“North stair, two Jaffa.”

The command net relayed the call.

“Vampires in the east hall. Don't shoot the windows; sunlight is doing half the work.”

Another call.

“Medical route moves to the gym exit. The courtyard is exposed.”

Nobody asked how she could fight and command.

They gave her information and carried out decisions.

The ground commander entered through the crater with twelve Jaffa and the master detonator attached to his wrist. His armor bore Cardea's hinge. Two soldiers carried the remaining charges toward the foundation.

O'Neill saw them through Cardea's own tactical network.

“Buffy, we have your bomb team.”

His voice arrived from inside the enemy Ha'tak, broken by weapons fire.

Carter transmitted the charge frequencies. “There are two armed devices and one control unit. I can interrupt the remote signal, but physical triggers remain live.”

“Do it.”

“Signal interrupted.”

The ground commander's wrist display went dark.

He tore it free and ordered his troops forward.

Buffy redirected the inner reserve.

She did not charge the commander because he looked important. She sent zat teams against the charge carriers, pulled the conventional line behind cover before staff fire reached it, and assigned the surrendered Jaffa advisers to call changes in formation.

Only when the commander broke through toward the basement did she meet him.

He carried a staff weapon in one hand and a short blade in the other. Cardea had chosen him for loyalty, strength, and the absence of imagination required to destroy what he could not possess.

“The hinge closes,” he said.

“You people really need new material.”

He fired.

Buffy went over the blast, struck the wall, and used the impact to reverse direction. Her sword caught the staff below its head. The commander twisted, trapped the blade, and drove his armored shoulder into her chest.

She hit the lockers hard enough to open three.

He advanced without pause.

Buffy saw his formation even while fighting him. Four Jaffa protected the route behind. Two were moving a charge by hand now that the remote system had failed. The rest held the reserve outside the corridor.

“Team two, ceiling pipe,” she ordered.

Rifle fire broke the steam line above the charge carriers. White vapor filled the corridor. Zat fire entered through it from the side. One carrier fell. The other dragged the device onward.

The commander attacked again.

Buffy gave ground until he believed she had none left.

Then she stopped fighting the armor.

She cut the straps holding it.

One shoulder plate fell. Then the breastplate shifted under its own weight. The commander's next strike pulled him off balance. Buffy turned the trapped sword, released it, and used both hands to drive the armor downward.

He struck the floor beneath it.

She kicked the blade away.

“Stay.”

He tried to rise.

Buffy put one foot between his shoulders and spoke into the radio.

“Commander down. Get the last charge.”

The remaining carrier reached the basement door.

Giles continued the invocation inside. Willow held the displaced key with both hands. The crack beneath the circle widened, showing a line of darkness too deep to belong under a school.

The Air Force guard at the door fired until his magazine emptied. The Jaffa armor held long enough for the warrior to raise the charge.

One of Teal'c's volunteer advisers struck him from behind.

The charge fell.

The adviser caught it.

“I do not know how to disarm this,” he said.

Carter answered from orbit. “Turn the upper ring counterclockwise to the third mark.”

He obeyed.

“Press the two recessed panels together.”

The device whined.

“Now remove the central crystal.”

“It does not release.”

“It will. Pull harder.”

The Jaffa braced one foot against the wall and pulled.

The crystal came free.

The whine stopped.

In the protected circle, Willow set the iron key into its original line.

Giles spoke the last word.

The darkness beneath the fracture closed.

Aboard Destiny, the two return coordinates became one.

“Mooring stable,” Xander said.

Buffy looked down at the captured commander beneath her foot.

The school shook with distant explosions. The cemetery still burned. People were injured. The battle above had not ended.

But the line held.
