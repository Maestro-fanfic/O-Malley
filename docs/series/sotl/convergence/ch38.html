What the Enemy Knows

The captured scout remembered every world it had watched.

Its archive contained navigational records, surveillance images, priestly reports, military orders, and lists of people who had passed through gates under the false Janus's control. The Goa'uld had preserved movement as other rulers preserved tribute. Every journey became proof that someone had crossed a threshold by his permission.

Carter, Teal'c, and Daniel worked aboard the scout while Rush and Brody extracted a parallel copy from Destiny. Wray observed every interrogation and insisted that captured personnel be distinguished from the data stored in their vessel.

The archive did not share her scruples.

It identified Jaffa by service number, symbiote maturity, medical utility, and likelihood of disobedience. Human populations appeared as labor pools and potential hosts. The scout's Goa'uld commander had listed his own host as equipment acquired seventy-three years earlier.

TJ read that entry once.

Then she went to the secured medical compartment where the host remained trapped inside his own body.

“Can he hear me?” she asked Teal'c.

“Almost certainly.”

The Goa'uld smiled with the host's mouth.

“He hears everything.”

TJ pulled a chair beside the bed.

“Then I'll explain his treatment to him too.”

The smile disappeared.

✳

The false Janus had monitored Earth intermittently since the eighteenth century.

He did not know where its Stargate had been buried. He did not know that Stargate Command operated beneath Cheyenne Mountain. He had inherited fragments of Ra's territorial records and maintained passive observation of the Solar System because Earth's rebellion had become a story Goa'uld told one another as a warning.

The observer changed every few decades. Some never transmitted. Others recorded radio development, aviation, nuclear detonations, and the opening of orbital space.

The current scout had arrived after detecting Goa'uld activity near Earth and remained because Destiny appeared before it could leave.

“It saw the ship arrive?” Hammond asked.

Carter answered from the briefing screen. “Not the transit itself. It entered the system several hours later. The first passive relay detected an energy event but couldn't classify it.”

“What did packet four reveal?”

“That an Ancient vessel is present and actively defending Earth. Possibly that it can survive stellar entry. The packet ended before the position and technical assessment.”

“Xander?”

“The scout associated his communications with Destiny, but his identity was in packet six.”

“Sunnydale?”

“Packet five.”

Hammond looked toward O'Neill.

“Ground transport?”

“No outbound transmission after deployment. We captured its communicator before the commander could report.”

Earth had kept its most important secrets.

It had not kept peace.

Daniel displayed the false Janus's standing directives.

“He collects Ancient devices associated with gates, navigation, stasis, and time. He believes passage is a form of sovereignty. If he learns that Destiny crossed years as well as distance, he won't treat it as a military curiosity.”

“He'll treat it as validation,” Giles said from Sunnydale. “Proof that the authority he claims can be made real.”

“And a threat,” Teal'c added. “A doorway beyond his command disproves him.”

Hammond considered the strategic map.

The scout's last complete records placed the false Janus four gate transits and several days by ship from Earth. He controlled one Ha'tak directly, another through an underlord called Cardea, and enough smaller vessels to overwhelm Earth's nonexistent space defense if Destiny were disabled or drawn away.

“Recommendations,” Hammond said.

Young spoke first.

“Assume attack. Repair weapons and shields. Wake only the personnel necessary to sustain defensive operations. Move the captured scout and transport under guard. Establish continuous sensor coverage beyond the Solar System.”

Carter added, “Search every Council record for Goa'uld devices or relay sites. Inspect the 1798 chamber in Italy. If Janus has another route into the system, we close it.”

O'Neill said, “Disperse the people who know about the anchor.”

Buffy looked at him through the Sunnydale feed.

“You mean us.”

“I mean don't put everyone the bad guy wants in the same library.”

“That is annoyingly sensible.”

“I practice.”

Hammond authorized the preparations.

He increased security around Sunnydale without placing the town under visible occupation. He ordered the SGC to develop evacuation and continuity plans. He notified the President that Earth had won its first engagement and might have announced itself to a new enemy in doing so.

Basil sent the report directly to the Queen.

Destiny began repairs.

Far beyond Earth, the escaped packet reached a Goa'uld relay disguised as a dead moon.

The relay forwarded the fragment toward the domain of Janus.

Someone else received it first.
