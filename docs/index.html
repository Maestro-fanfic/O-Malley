Maestro's Fanfic Archive

a home for the stories, series by series

Watch as he...

Tries obsessively, for the better part of a century, to write believable prose of any kind

Fails miserably, time after time

Attends workshops, seminars, even one-on-ones with published authors

Keeps failing miserably

And finally, after decades, concludes he's just incapable of doing it. “I mean, I had to get there eventually.”

Marvel as he...

Breaks every rule made by man, nature, physics, and common decency, just to make his obsession bear fruit he'll actually enjoy reading

Uses a personal mantra as his mission statement. “Well, yeah. ‘Find the funny’ is kind of catchy when you think about it.”

Mocks everything in the universe: things that are real, things that are made up, characters from any fandom that catches his interest, the actors who play them, and… himself

Featuring him...

Picking up a tool that draws nothing but pitchforks from half the writing community

Not giving a single fuck about any of it

Defending that choice loudly, repeatedly, at a length nobody asked for

Refusing, categorically, to back down

Starring...

One (1) revolving door

One entire cast encompassing every character that ever existed, passing through it… and a few that only existed in a fever-dream

One (?) Omnipotent Being

One (1) all-time favorite author, Steven Gould, without whom none of this would exist

and

One intent to mock anyone who disagrees with that opinion

Be astounded by...

A writing style that imitates someone who imitates something else already

(Seriously, those guys over at Screen Junkies are fucking incredible. Major props to them.)

Series

  • O'Make Way, O'Malley!

    an anthology of passenger-seat crossovers, various fandoms

    ROB O'Make O'Malley drops a person or fictional character into someone else's mind as a permanent passenger. Every entry is its own standalone pairing, its own continuity, its own fandom crossover.

Standalones

  • Ship of the Line - Convergence

    Buffy the Vampire Slayer x Stargate SG-1 x Stargate Universe x No Man's Sky x The West Wing

    Complete (27 chapters)

    On Halloween, three costumes stop being costumes. Xander wakes up carrying Eli Wallace's memories and a future he hasn't lived yet; Buffy and Willow wake up not human anymore, at all, permanently. A dare from a costume shop turns into first contact with two governments, an Ancient warship, and whatever's left of who they used to be.

  • Wood It Work: Book 2 — Wardrobes and Would-Work

    Buffy the Vampire Slayer x Dungeons & Dragons

    Complete (57 chapters, numbered 26–82)

    A continuation of dogbertcarroll's Wood It Work, picking up at Chapter 26. Xander, Willow, Jesse, and the household built around one very unusual workshop door keep finding new worlds on the other side of it, including a water-scarce desert culture mid-treaty negotiation and whatever King Then'tal's court actually wants from them.
